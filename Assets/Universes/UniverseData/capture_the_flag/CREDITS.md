Everything below is free for commercial use, contains no executables, and is used from the copies
already in this project (nothing new was downloaded for this game).

Credit is not required for the CC0 assets; it is given here anyway. Sandbag and base-concrete
textures are generated by `Editor/CTF_Authoring.cs`.

numbers and the post-processing mood come from the shared game-feel kit
(`Packages/com.ryan.gameframework/Runtime/GameFeel`, Kenney CC0 sounds credited in its `Audio/CREDITS.md`).
